# ClawBio Boston - Agents for Science — NVIDIA

**Attendee Setup Guide**

A few quick steps: create a Brev account, add your workshop credits, and launch the hands-on labs. This takes about five minutes.

---

## Step 1 — Create your Brev account

Brev is the platform where you'll run every lab in this hackathon. Open the Brev console and sign up (or sign in) with your email.

👉 **[Sign up at brev.nvidia.com](https://brev.nvidia.com/)**

---

## Step 2 — Add your hackathon credits

Your labs run on GPU credits. Redeem the coupon code we provided so your account is funded before you launch anything. Unused credits expire on **October 1, 2026**.

1. In the Brev console, open the **Billing** tab in the top navigation.
2. Under **2. Credits**, click **Redeem Code**.
3. Enter the code from your hackathon handout (format `clawbio-bos-XXXXXX`) and click **Redeem**. Your balance updates immediately.

> **Tip:** Don't have the code? Ask a hackathon facilitator — it's printed on your handout.

---

## Step 3 — Launch the labs

Once your credits are added, launch each lab directly from the links below. Each opens a ready-to-run environment in Brev.

| Lab | Title | Link |
|-----|-------|------|
| Lab 1 | Run Qwen3.6-27B-FP8 on 1×RTX Pro 6000 | [Launch Lab 1](https://brev.nvidia.com/launchable/deploy?launchableID=env-3GPBDpmGQwKUiQ5VlxpmA19yKEH) |

> **Tip:** GPU not available? Before deploying, edit the launchable's compute settings to pick a **different provider**, or a **different instance type** with another GPU. Choose a GPU with at least as much memory as the original.

---

## Step 4 — Clean up!

> **Important:** When you're done, **delete your instances**. If you only stop them, they will **continue to accrue charges**.

In the Brev console, open each instance you launched and click **Delete** — not just Stop. Repeat for every lab environment you started.
